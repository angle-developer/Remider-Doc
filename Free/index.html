Introduction

Reminder Robo is a paid Android application designed to help users manage reminders, tasks, bills, EMIs, important dates, and daily activities.

We respect your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the app handles information and uses device permissions to provide its features.

2

Information Storage

Reminder Robo stores reminder information and app data locally on your device using an on-device database.

Information you enter may include:

  • Reminder titles and descriptions.
  • Task details and completion status.
  • Dates, times, and repeat schedules.
  • Bill and EMI reminders.
  • Important dates and personal notes.
  • App preferences and settings.

🔒 Your reminder data is not transmitted to our servers because Reminder Robo does not use a developer-operated cloud storage service.

The app does not use advertising SDKs, analytics services, or crash-reporting services.

Please note that Android device backups or other device-level services may handle app data according to their own settings and policies.

3

Use of Information

Information stored by the app is used to provide its core functionality, including:

  • Creating and managing reminders.
  • Scheduling alarms and notifications.
  • Displaying tasks and important dates.
  • Saving app preferences.
  • Supporting optional features that you choose to use.

We do not sell your personal information.

4

Device Permissions

Reminder Robo may request the following permissions to support its features:

📷
Camera

Camera access may be used when you choose to capture a photo to attach to a reminder or use another camera-related feature. The camera is not intended to be used for background surveillance.

🖼️
Photos & Gallery

Photo or gallery access may be used when you choose an image to attach to a reminder or personalize content within the app. Selected images are used for the feature you request. The app does not intentionally upload these images to a developer-operated server.

🔔
Notifications

Notification permission allows Reminder Robo to display reminders, task alerts, and other notifications you enable. If you deny notification permission, some reminder alerts may not appear.

⏰
Full-Screen Alarms

Where supported and permitted by Android, the app may use full-screen notifications to display important scheduled alarms or reminders prominently over the lock screen. You can manage applicable permissions through your device settings.

5

Data Sharing

Reminder Robo does not operate its own server to collect or share your reminder information.

The app does not integrate advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting services.

However, Android, your device manufacturer, or system backup services may process certain information independently according to their own functionality and privacy policies.

If you deliberately share content through another application, that application's privacy practices will apply to the information you share.

6

Data Security

Reminder Robo stores its application data locally on your device.

We recommend protecting your device with a screen lock and keeping your operating system up to date.

No method of electronic storage is completely risk-free. The security of locally stored information may also depend on your device's security settings and backup configuration.

7

Data Retention and Deletion

Your reminder information remains on your device until you delete it or the relevant application data is removed.

  • You can delete reminders within the app using the available controls.
  • Uninstalling the app or clearing its data may remove locally stored information. Recovery may not be possible unless a compatible backup exists.
  • Device backups, if enabled, may retain app data according to your device settings and backup provider's policies.
8

Paid Application

Reminder Robo is offered as a paid application.

The app does not display advertisements and does not use advertising SDKs.

Purchases made through Google Play are subject to Google's applicable payment, privacy, and purchase policies. We do not receive your full payment card details through the app.

9

Children's Privacy

Reminder Robo is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable laws.

Because reminder information is stored locally, users should exercise care when entering personal or sensitive details into the app.

If you have concerns about the handling of personal information, please contact us using the details below.

10

Your Choices

You can:

  • Choose what information to enter into reminders.
  • Edit or delete reminders.
  • Grant or deny optional device permissions.
  • Manage notification settings.
  • Remove the app and its locally stored data, subject to applicable backup behavior.

Some features may not work as intended if the permissions they require are denied.
