Introduction

Welcome to ReminderRobo Lite, a free Android application developed by LOKMI TECH ("we", "our", or "us"). ReminderRobo Lite is designed to help you create, organize, and manage reminders, to-do lists, bills, EMIs, alarms, and daily tasks with ease.

We respect your privacy and are committed to transparency. This Privacy Policy details how ReminderRobo Lite handles your personal data, why certain device permissions are requested, and how trusted third-party SDKs—namely Google AdMob, Google Analytics for Firebase, and Firebase Crashlytics—are utilized to support and maintain this free edition.

By downloading, installing, or using ReminderRobo Lite, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

2

Information You Enter & On-Device Storage

ReminderRobo Lite operates on an on-device storage architecture for your personal reminders and tasks. All content you input into the application remains stored locally in a private database on your Android device.

Information you create or save locally may include:

  • Reminder titles, notes, descriptions, and checklists.
  • Scheduled dates, reminder times, and recurrence frequencies (daily, weekly, monthly).
  • Bill amounts, EMI dues, and payment target dates.
  • Important event dates, birthdays, and personal tags.
  • Photos or images you choose to attach locally to a reminder.
  • Custom in-app preferences and notification settings.

🔒 Your personal reminder and task data is stored locally on your device and is NOT uploaded to our developer servers. We do not operate a proprietary remote cloud database to access, read, or monetize your reminder content.

Please note that if you have Android system backup or Google Drive device backup enabled on your phone, your device operating system may include application data in automated backups in accordance with Google's backup policies and your device configuration.

Device Permissions

ReminderRobo Lite requires specific Android device permissions to deliver reminders on time, enable multimedia attachments, and support advertising and diagnostic features:

🔔
Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS)

Enables the app to display alerts, status updates, and reminder banners when your scheduled alarms trigger on Android 13 (API 33) and newer.

⏰
Exact Alarms & Full Screen

Allows the app to schedule alarms with exact precision (SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM / USE_EXACT_ALARM) and display full-screen wake-up alerts over the lock screen for critical tasks.

🌐
Internet & Network Access

android.permission.INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE allow Google AdMob to load advertisements, Firebase Crashlytics to transmit error logs, and Google Analytics to transmit app usage metrics.

🆔
Advertising ID (AD_ID)

com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID allows Google Play Services to provide the device advertising identifier required for ad delivery, fraud prevention, and frequency capping on Android 13+.

📷
Camera

Optional permission used solely if you choose to take a photo to attach directly to a reminder or task note. Captured images remain strictly on your local device.

🖼️
Photos & Media

Optional permission utilized when you choose an existing photo or attachment from your gallery to associate with a reminder. Selected files are referenced locally.

Free Edition & Advertising Choices (Google AdMob)

ReminderRobo Lite is a free application. Advertisements served through Google AdMob allow us to offer all core reminder functionalities free of charge without requiring a subscription.

Depending on your region, consent selections, and device settings, AdMob may display either personalized ads (tailored to your interests based on previous activity across apps) or non-personalized ads (contextual ads based on device information, coarse location, or content within the app).

⚙️ You have full control over advertising personalization. You can reset or delete your Google Advertising ID, or opt out of personalized advertisements at any time through your Android device settings.

How to manage or reset your Advertising ID on Android:

  1. Open your device's Settings app.
  2. Scroll down and tap Google (or Privacy → Ads on some Android versions).
  3. Select Ads.
  4. Tap Reset advertising ID to disconnect prior ad associations, or tap Delete advertising ID to remove the ID altogether.
  5. You may also toggle Opt out of Ads Personalization if available on your Android version.

You can also manage your Google ad personalization preferences online by visiting Google Ads Settings.

Data Sharing & Third-Party Disclosure

We value your trust and maintain strict guidelines regarding data disclosure:

  • We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal reminder data. Your reminder titles, schedules, task notes, and attachments are private and never monetized.
  • Third-Party Service Providers: As detailed in Section 3, technical device data, crash logs, analytics events, and advertising identifiers are processed by Google LLC (via AdMob, Analytics, and Crashlytics) exclusively to support app operations, stability, and monetization.
  • Legal Compliance: We may disclose information only if required by law, court order, or official governmental regulation, or to protect our legal rights, safety, or property.
  • User-Initiated Sharing: If you use Android's native share feature to send a reminder or task to another app (e.g., messaging, email, notes), the privacy policy of that recipient application governs that content.

Data Security

We adopt reasonable security measures to safeguard information:

  • Sandboxed Local Storage: Reminder data is saved inside Android's protected application sandbox, inaccessible to other standard apps without system-level elevation.
  • Encrypted Transmission: Diagnostic crash reports, analytics telemetry, and ad requests sent to Google servers are transmitted over secure, encrypted HTTPS/TLS network protocols.
  • User Precautions: Because your reminders reside on your phone, we encourage you to protect your device with a secure lock screen (PIN, pattern, password, or biometrics) and maintain up-to-date system software.
8

Data Retention & Deletion

You have direct control over the retention and deletion of your data:

  • In-App Task Deletion: You can edit or delete individual reminders, checklists, or categories at any time from within the app interface.
  • Clear All App Data: You can permanently wipe all locally stored reminders, photos, and settings by navigating to Settings → Apps → ReminderRobo Lite → Storage → Clear Storage / Clear Data.
  • Uninstalling the App: Uninstalling ReminderRobo Lite removes the local database and cached files from your device (unless preserved by an external system backup).
  • Diagnostic & Analytics Retention: Firebase Crashlytics retains crash event logs for up to 90 days. Google Analytics retains aggregated usage data in accordance with Google's default retention standards.
